Our Vision

Marwadi University aims to be a leading global institution recognized for excellence in education, research, and innovation, cultivating a community of learners and leaders to contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

  • Provide high-quality education that fosters critical thinking and creativity.
  • Promote research, innovation, and industry collaboration.
  • Encourage ethical values and social responsibility among students.

Courses Offered

B.Tech

Computer, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and Chemical Engineering, along with ICT and specialized fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, Cloud Computing, and Cyber Security.

Law

Law offers an integrated B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) and B.Com. LL.B. (Hons.) five-year programs, an LL.M. program with specializations, and a Ph.D. in Law.

B.Pharm and B.Sc

B.Pharm and M.Pharm (Pharmaceutics, Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs), B.Sc Nursing, and B.Sc in Chemistry, Microbiology, Biotechnology, Physics, Mathematics, Agriculture, and Environmental Sciences. Ph.D. programs are also available in these disciplines.

Courses Outcomes

Marwadi University's course outcomes focus on industry-ready graduates through a blend of strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and soft skill development, aiming to make students future-ready with a focus on entrepreneurship and societal impact.

Foundational Mastery: Graduates will possess a comprehensive and deep understanding of core subject principles, mathematical concepts, and fundamental scientific theories necessary for innovation and advanced studies. They are trained to analyze complex problems using established academic frameworks.

Industry Readiness: Students are proficient in utilizing modern tools, techniques, and software relevant to their field. They can apply theoretical knowledge directly to solve real-world industrial and societal challenges through hands-on laboratory work, project-based learning, and internships.

Future-Ready Mindset: Emphasis is placed on developing effective communication, teamwork, and leadership skills. Graduates are also instilled with a focus on entrepreneurship, ethical responsibility, and the understanding of the broader societal and environmental implications of their professional work.
